Q1For festivals

Lighting for
festivals.

Multiple stages, changing acts and a tight timetable. We make sure every stage has its own face and that the show stands on every stage.

Q2Approach

At a festival everything comes together: lots of artists back to back, short changeovers and little time to programme. That's why we prepare the show in advance wherever we can, and on the day we know exactly what each act needs.

We deliver the lighting design for a stage, programme and run the shows, or step in as operator on an existing rig. We bring visuals and LED along too, so light and image form one whole.

  • Lighting design per stage
  • Programming in advance, in 3D where possible
  • Operators for every act
  • Visuals and LED in the same line
