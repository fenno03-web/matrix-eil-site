Q2.5The story

A hundred years, celebrated big.

Sportclub’25 from Bocholtz turned a hundred, and that had to be celebrated in style. With a line-up including Jan Smit, Emma Heesters, Dries Roelvink and Miljo, the goal was clear: go big and put GTF Franzen Sound Light Vision firmly back on the map.

A line-up like that calls for a show just as big. We drew the stage and lighting design and ended up using more than 130 fixtures, complemented by video and special effects. We did the programming & live operation ourselves, so every act got exactly the light that suited it.

It turned into a party full of highlights. A party in the tent with Jan Smit, Dries Roelvink, and a Monday night we closed with Miljo: goosebumps. To this day we still get compliments about it.
