ARGUS Metron beta

The DJ’s tempo, straight into your lighting console. METRON reads the Pioneer CDJs over Pro DJ Link or listens to the audio, and sends the BPM directly to your grandMA3 or Chimp. You see phrases and drops coming, and connect further with Ableton Link, MIDI and OSC. For Windows and Mac.

  • Pioneer CDJ / Pro DJ Link
  • Audio analysis
  • grandMA3
  • Infinity Chimp
  • Phrases & drops
  • Ableton Link
  • MIDI
  • OSC
  • Windows
  • Mac
