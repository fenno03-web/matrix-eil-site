Q2What we do

A familiar setup is quickly chosen. Yet every production deserves the question again: why does this fit here? We look at the space, the sightlines, the programme and the available time and resources, and translate that into a design where every element has a reason.

Because programming and live operation are in the same hands with us, we know how a design will behave during the show. So what was conceived on paper really ends up on stage that way.

  • Concept and atmosphere for stage and lighting
  • Rig design, tuned to space, time and budget
  • Coordination with production and rental partners
  • Programming and operation by the same team
