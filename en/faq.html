Questions
from the floor.

The questions we get most often, with an honest answer. Is your question not here? Feel free to send it.

Q01What exactly does MATRIX E.I.L. do?

We take care of the visual picture of live productions: stage and lighting design, lighting programming, live operation, VJ and visuals, and LED technology. Separately, or all in one hand.

Q02What does a lighting design or an operator cost?

That depends on the size of the stage, the number of show days and how much preparation is needed. Send us your date, location and what you have in mind, and you'll quickly get a tailored proposal.

Q03How far in advance should I book?

The sooner the better, especially in festival and carnival season. For a design we like a few weeks to months of preparation. An operator on an existing rig is sometimes possible at short notice too, just ask.

Q04Do you supply the equipment as well?

That varies per production. We often work with rental and technical companies who supply the equipment, while we take care of the design, the programming and the show. We're happy to think along about the best solution.

Q05Which lighting consoles do you work on?

Mainly grandMA3 and Infinity Chimp. For video we work with Resolume, and we build shows in advance in Capture. Read more about our operators.

Q06Do you only work in Limburg?

We work a lot in Limburg, but we run shows across the rest of the country just as well, from Paaspop and Pinkpop to Decibel Outdoor.

Q07Can you do just the programming, without a design?

Certainly. We step in on an existing design or rig, programme the show and run it live. Or we programme in advance and hand the show file over to your own operator.

Q08What is ARGUS Systems?

ARGUS Systems is the software branch of MATRIX. We build tools for production and show operators, born from problems we ran into on the floor ourselves. Take a look at ARGUS.

Q09How do I get in touch?

Fastest via WhatsApp or email to info@matrixeventsupport.nl. You can also find us on Instagram.
