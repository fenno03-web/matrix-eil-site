Planning, warehouse and show in one system.

AXIS is a planning and warehouse platform for the events industry, built from modules. Beyond planning and equipment management, AXIS also brings the production itself under one roof: from driving screens to accreditation. So it grows with what you need.

  • Planning & warehouse: projects, crew and equipment in one place
  • LIVE: control screens and zones during the event
  • Accreditation: professional accreditation, within reach for smaller events too
