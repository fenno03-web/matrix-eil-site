Q2What we do

A good show starts long before the first act takes the stage. We prepare the show on the lighting console, build looks and cues that suit the artists and the programme, and make sure everything is ready when the doors open.

During the show we're behind the console ourselves. We respond to what happens: to a DJ who plays differently than planned, to a crowd that wants more, to the moment that asks for just a bit more or just a bit less.

  • Preparing and programming the show on the lighting console
  • Live operation during the show
  • Responding to DJs, live acts and the crowd
  • Hired by productions and rental companies
