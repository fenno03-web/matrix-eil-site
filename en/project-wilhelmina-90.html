Q2.5The story

A gym you won't recognise.

Gymnastics club Wilhelmina turned ninety and celebrated with a three-day party. GTF Franzen Sound Light Vision took care of the technology, MATRIX of the stage and lighting design. The idea: make an ordinary gym feel like a real party venue. You had to walk in and briefly forget where you were.

The biggest challenge was the venue itself. The gym was on the first floor, so all the equipment was unloaded at the back in a field and carried up from there.

The evening opened with a timecode light show: a small showcase of what MATRIX can do, with light running exactly to the music. The best moment came when everything was in place and the hall really no longer looked like a standard sports hall.
