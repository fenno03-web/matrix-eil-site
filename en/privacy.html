Privacy

MATRIX E.I.L. only collects personal data through this website when you enter it yourself, such as in a beta sign-up. No tracking or advertising cookies are used. If you contact us by email or phone, we only use your details to answer your message.

If you sign up as a beta tester for an ARGUS tool, your name, email address and answers are sent to our email address via the form service FormSubmit. We only use that data to contact you about the beta and keep it no longer than necessary. Want us to delete it? Email us.

Fonts are loaded via Google Fonts. In the process, your IP address is processed by Google.

Questions about privacy? Email info@matrixeventsupport.nl.
