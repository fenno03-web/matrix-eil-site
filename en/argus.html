Alpha · in the field

One clock for the whole production.

Show control, backstage, dressing rooms and FOH see the same time live. On every screen, without internet. CHRONOS tracks every stage, shows overruns instantly and makes sure everyone knows who is on now and for how long.

  • All stages side by side: live, changeover, who is next and the margin to curfew
  • Overruns and new start times straight into the timeline; import the timetable from PDF, photo or Excel
  • Screens for backstage, dressing rooms and crew room, with messages from show control
  • Artists and crew follow their own times via one link on their phone
  • Fully offline, no accounts or cloud, on your own production network

In testing

Sight of the music. Grip on the moment.

METRON is a musical information and timing environment for lighting operators. It combines Pro DJ Link and BPM detection from audio, and passes the tempo straight to the speed masters on your lighting console. The operator decides when METRON takes over. That shifts the focus from tapping to shaping the show.

  • Tempo from Pro DJ Link or audio, straight to your speed masters
  • Insight into decks and tracks
  • Your own show markers with a countdown to key moments
  • Musical timing calculations for fades and delays

04
In testing

Know what's on your route before you leave.

HOROS is a navigation app for anyone driving a van or truck from production to production. Using up-to-date RDW data, HOROS warns you about low-emission zones and, once you enter your vehicle's dimensions, about bridges and viaducts you won't fit under.

  • Live navigation: alerts on the road, while you drive
  • Route check: have the whole route checked for bottlenecks in advance
  • Location check: quickly see whether an address is reachable with your vehicle

Seven new tools, from a vault for show files to followspots that follow by themselves. Each born from a problem we ran into on the floor.

In development · live testing

The bridge between consoles, with a watcher looking over your shoulder.

BIFROST links lighting consoles together and keeps an eye on everything during the show. Heimdall, the built-in AI assistant, spots deviations straight away and helps you when it counts.

  • Console bridge between desks
  • A watcher guarding the show
  • Heimdall AI as a helper next to the console

In development

Aim your fixtures from the floor.

Position fixtures on site wirelessly with a handheld, such as an MSI Claw. You point to where the light should go and the fixtures follow. That makes programming faster and more precise.

  • Wireless from a handheld
  • Aim fixtures by pointing
  • Programme positions and focus faster

A lighting console in your laptop.

For small events without the budget for a real console or lighting designer. HYPERION reads MVR files and turns a PC or laptop into a clear lighting tool.

  • Reads MVR files

  • Made for small events

Macros for the Infinity Chimp.

A macro tool for the Highlite Infinity Chimp G2 and G3. Build a sequence of actions once, then call it up with a single press.
