Q2Hire an operatorLighting · Video

MATRIX provides lighting programmers, lighting operators and VJs for festivals, events and tours. We step into your rig and your design, or bring the design ourselves.

We come prepared: the showfile is ready in advance where possible, we’ve been through the timetable and we know who your production contact is. On the day we run the show in the moment, with an eye for the artist and the audience.

Q3Operator riderWhat we need

The rider
for the desk.

  1. 01

    A spot at FOH

    With a clear view of the stage and the screens.

  2. 02

    Power

    A dedicated, clean circuit for desk and laptop.

  3. 03

    Data

    Network or DMX all the way to FOH, ideally with a spare line.

  4. 04

    Info in advance

    Timetable, rig plot, patch and the production contact, a few days before the show.

  5. 05

    Time to focus

    A dark moment during the build to set positions and focus.

  6. 06

    Crew catering

    Food and drinks. An operator on an empty stomach misses cues.
