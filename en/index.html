Q3.5How we workFor organisers

One partner,
one picture.

Design, programming, visuals and LED in one hand. Less coordination, and the picture on your stage tells one story.

  1. 01

    Introduction

    We talk through your event: the venue, the audience, the programme and what you want people to feel.

  2. 02

    Design

    A plan for stage, lighting, visuals and LED that fits the space, the time and the budget.

  3. 03

    Preparation

    Programming, creating content and coordinating with your production and technical partners.

  4. 04

    Show

    We run the desk ourselves, so the design on stage really looks the way it was planned.

Q4 · Manifest

It only exists
for a moment.

“It ever was, is now, and ever shall be an ever-living fire.”Heraclitus

Months of preparation for a moment that never repeats itself exactly. The same space, the same music, the same technology, and yet something different every time.

That is what fascinates us about live productions. You can prepare everything, but only with people in the room do you find out what it becomes.

MATRIX shapes the conditions for that moment. With ideas, technology and people who know their craft. What it finally means happens between the stage and the audience.
