Q1Glossary32 terms

Jargon,
translated.

On the floor we talk in abbreviations. Here's what they mean, from Art-Net to wash.

ABCDFGHILMOPRSTVW

A

Art-Net
A protocol for sending DMX data over a normal network. That way many universes travel over one cable.

B

Beam
A fixture with a narrow, tight light beam. Clearly visible in haze, for sharp lines in the air.
Blinder
A bright light aimed at the audience, for short moments of blinding at a drop or climax.
Busking
Running lights live without fixed cues, on feel and to the music. Often used for dance acts.

C

Capture
Software for building and programming a light show in 3D in advance, so you save time on site.
Changeover
The changeover between two acts on the same stage.
Cue
A stored moment in the show: which fixtures are on, in which colour, position and intensity.
Cue list
A series of cues in a row, which you play one by one with GO.

D

DMX
The standard control signal for lighting. One DMX universe drives up to 512 channels.

F

FOH
Front of house: the place in the venue where lighting, sound and video are operated.
Fixture
A light or device you control from the lighting console.

G

grandMA3
A widely used lighting console for large shows and festivals.

H

Haze
Fine mist that makes light beams visible in the air.

I

Infinity Chimp
A lighting console by Highlite, fast and flexible for stages and tours.

L

LD
Lighting designer: the person who decides what the show looks like.
LED wall
A large screen built from LED panels, for visuals and live footage.

M

Moving head
A light with a rotating head that you can aim, colour and move remotely.
MVR
My Virtual Rig: a file format for exchanging a complete rig with positions and patch between programs.

O

Operator
The person who operates the lighting console or video during the show.

P

Patch
The link between the fixtures and their DMX addresses, so the console knows which fixture is where.
Pixel pitch
The distance between the pixels of an LED screen. The smaller, the sharper the image up close.
Pixelmapping
Spreading video or effects across individual fixtures or LED strips as if they were the pixels of a screen.
Programmer
The person who builds the show in the lighting console: cues, effects and palettes.

R

Resolume
Software for live visuals and VJ, widely used at festivals and in clubs.

S

sACN
A network protocol for DMX data, just like Art-Net.
Speedmaster
A control on the lighting console that sets the speed of effects to the tempo of the music.
Strobe
A light that flashes very briefly and brightly.

T

Timecode
A time signal that keeps lighting, video and music exactly in sync.
Truss
The aluminium structure that fixtures, screens and speakers hang from.

V

VJ
Video jockey: creates and mixes live visuals to the rhythm of the music.
Followspot
A light that follows an artist across the stage, by hand or automatically.

W

Wash
A light with a wide, soft beam for colouring surfaces and people.
