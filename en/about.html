Article§1

Origin

MATRIX E.I.L. started from the wish to work independently and grow as a freelancer. Along the way it became ever clearer which way of working belongs to that, and which conviction lies behind it.

Every event deserves attention for its own character. The venue, the audience and the occasion call for new choices every time. That calls for a design with thought and effort behind it.

From that conviction, MATRIX brings together stage and lighting design, programming, visuals and LED technology. With care for the idea and for the execution.

Article§2

A design should be about something.

A familiar setup is quickly chosen. Yet every production deserves the question again: why does this fit here?

For MATRIX that question is part of the craft. Looking at the space, exploring options and thinking beyond the first solution. The available time and resources set the limits; within them there is room for deliberate choices.

A simple set can be exactly right. The difference lies in the thinking behind it. Why is something there? Where does the attention go? What does it add to the experience?

Light, image and space influence each other. Darkness can give a movement meaning. A screen can create depth, but can also claim all the attention. The meaning of each element comes from how it relates to the rest.

That coherence deserves care, from the first sketch to the last cue.

Article§3

A signature of its own. Many perspectives.

MATRIX has a personal beginning. The ambition is to build on that base with independent specialists who bring their own knowledge, ideas and judgement.

A shared conviction doesn’t have to mean an identical view. Someone who asks a different question is exactly the one who can take a design further. That takes trust, clear agreements and room for real discussion.

What we want to share is commitment to the result. Understanding why a choice is made. Paying attention to each other’s work. And taking the trouble to carry out an idea with care.
